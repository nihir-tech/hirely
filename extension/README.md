# Hirely — LinkedIn Job Copilot & Resume Matcher (Chrome / Edge Extension)

Bring Hirely's AI job matching, ATS skill-gap detection, recruiter pitch writer, and interview prep directly onto LinkedIn.

## Features
- **Live LinkedIn Job Match Score:** Automatically calculates a match score (`0–99%`), highlights matched vs. missing skills, and adds quick fit badges onto LinkedIn job search cards.
- **Smart LinkedIn Job Finder Presets:** 1-click LinkedIn searches tuned for Startups & Small Teams, Early-Bird (`<10` applicants), Fresh (`Past 24h`), and Remote + Easy Apply—pre-filled from your active Hirely resume.
- **1-Click Pipeline, Resume Tailor & Interview Prep:** Save jobs directly to your Hirely Dashboard Kanban board, open the Resume Optimizer pre-filled with the LinkedIn job description, inspect Skill Gap Roadmaps, or generate tailored Mock Interview questions.
- **AI Recruiter Note & Cover Pitch Writer:** Generates a ready-to-send `<300` char LinkedIn connection note and tailored hiring manager InMail on any job post.
- **1-Click LinkedIn Profile Import:** Visit your profile on `linkedin.com/in/...` and import your experience, education, and skills directly into Hirely for ATS scoring and PDF export.
- **Auto-Sync with Hirely:** Automatically syncs with your saved resumes whenever you visit `hirelly.vercel.app` (or includes an instant paste fallback inside the extension popup).

## How to Install Locally (Chrome / Edge / Brave)
1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions` in Edge).
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Select this `extension/` folder (`f:\hirelly\extension`).
5. Pin **Hirely — LinkedIn Job Copilot** to your browser toolbar and visit `linkedin.com/jobs`!
